# AI ENGINEER CASE STUDY

## Introduction

You have made it this far, and now it is time to demonstrate your skills!

Your mission is to create a functional Generative AI solution for a real business case for a company that has hired you. You must design, validate, and implement this solution. Be sure to address all topics mentioned in the requirements.

On the presentation day, ensure the complete end-to-end functionality of the case study for the evaluation committee. Explain the proposed architecture, justify your technological choices, and provide practical examples of how each aspect of the solution would be implemented.

You may use a detailed presentation as a guide, including diagrams, documentation, and explanations of each solution component. Provide an implementation plan and consider future challenges and enhancements. Remember that the solution should be scalable, secure, and efficient.

The domain of the case study, regarding the data used, is entirely up to the candidate. Examples include:

- Public healthcare
- Stock market analysis
- Demographic or social studies
- Commodities
- Any other relevant topic

---

# Possible Implementation Themes

You may choose one of the following themes:

## 1. Assistance and Interaction

- Create chatbots, intelligent FAQs, or internal assistants.
- Focus: improve customer service experience and user interaction.

## 2. Automation and Knowledge Extraction

- Build workflows that read, organize, classify, or summarize information.
- Focus: increase efficiency and reduce manual effort in analyzing documents, reports, or contracts.

## 3. Technical Support and Productivity

- Develop agents that assist with technical tasks such as:
  - Code generation
  - Log analysis
  - Support for IT and data teams

- Focus: improve productivity and automate complex activities.

---

# Required Topics

You must design and implement a functional Generative AI solution that includes:

- The use of one or more AI models capable of solving the identified business problem.

- The selection of prompts (if applicable) and their orchestration strategy to ensure controlled, consistent, and reliable responses.

- The use of fictional company data (for example:
  - FAQ repositories
  - Product information
  - Return policies
  - Any other information relevant to the problem)

  The solution must explain how the data is:
  - Prepared or generated
  - Stored
  - Consumed by the application

- A language model strategy such as:
  - Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
  - Fine-tuning
  - Any alternative approach

  Also explain how the solution’s performance will be evaluated.

- A complete AI application architecture design involving models and/or agents, including key decisions and trade-offs.

- The assumptions, analyses, and techniques applied to ensure:
  - Ethics
  - Privacy
  - Responsible AI practices

  Consider the covered scenarios and explain how the selected solutions address the identified needs.

# General Recommendations

## Technology Justification

When selecting techniques and technologies for the project, it is essential to justify your choices using solid criteria.

Explain why you selected specific models, tools, and solutions, highlighting how they align with the project's requirements.

Use concrete examples to demonstrate how the chosen components contribute to the effectiveness of the solution.

---

## Solution Reproducibility

The candidate must ensure that:

- The implemented solution can be reproduced on another machine or environment.
- All configuration, installation, and execution scripts are included in the Git repository.
- Clear instructions are provided for configuring and running the project on another machine or environment.
